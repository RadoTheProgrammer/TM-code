For each student: a = current TM preference; b = sum of remaining TM preferences; a/b = base ratio. student_weight is a/b, optionally multiplied by (nproblems_eleves[student] + 1) when student weighting is enabled. For a duo, duo_weight is the product of member weights and candidate_weight is assigned to the representative; sampling_weight is candidate_weight raised to PUISSANCE_ELEVE when a weighted draw is used. sampling_probability is the initial probability for that draw; selected candidates are sampled without replacement, so later probabilities change. selection_order shows assignment order.
